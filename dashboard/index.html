Data rules and limitations

Order-line keys are unique. Order-level dimensions must agree before ingestion commits. Repeated identical files are skipped using SHA-256 manifests; changed batches upsert by order-item ID. Completed orders are COMPLETE/CLOSED only. No names, emails, passwords, street addresses or precise coordinates are loaded or published. Deletions require a governed snapshot process; incremental upserts do not infer deleted records.
